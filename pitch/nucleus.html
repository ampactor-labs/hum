Appendix

The trusted base

  • The compiler is written in Mentl and the pinned binary reproduces itself byte for byte; that fixed point is the release.
  • The verifier is sound for what it decides and incomplete by design: effects and types are judged by check; a bound it cannot decide is listed as pending by audit and printed on every run.
  • One false proof is known and documented: a recursion deep enough to exhaust the stack traps although its function says it cannot.
  • Rust prevents memory bugs, not effects; Simulink and SCADE verify models and generate C. Neither states "never reaches the network" as an obligation through every callee, proves a function allocation-free, or derives a learning rule from the chain.

Appendix

Running on a controller

  • The proof is at compile time. The artifact is one WebAssembly module importing WASI and nothing else.
  • It runs wherever wasmtime runs, which includes the Linux supervisors that sit beside inverters today; the demo's controller runs that way and answers each reading in under a millisecond.
  • A native backend is on the roadmap; the pedal in the demo runs the ear natively on a Cortex-M7 today.
  • The learner keeps learning inside a loop proven not to allocate: learning at the edge that cannot stall.
  • A running computation is a value: the compiler's own resume verb restores a persisted image and continues, so durability is construction, not infrastructure.
