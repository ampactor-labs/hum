The eara guitar pickup, a cable, a room

A pickup is a coil. Every wire in this room hums at sixty.

The hum's pitch is not the room's. It belongs to the whole Western Interconnection, Alberta to Baja. It sags when the grid is short.

The grid is a note. Sixty hertz is a B, forty-nine cents flat. One cent is thirty-five millihertz, so the grid's whole quiet life is the tenths digit on a tuner.

Read to the millihertz, three independent ways that agree, with the DSP library behind my pedals.

The same code runs on a thirty-dollar chip inside a guitar pedal.

HUM · 2 / 5a pickup · a pedal · sonido

Two bandsa Station answers at two speeds

Spin takes the milliseconds. A plant trips, the frequency falls, Spin answers within a third of a second. The reflex is a compressor on the grid's pitch: the deadband is its threshold, the droop its ratio, ten C its ceiling.

Pulse takes the hours. The rush hour is on the page before it arrives; Pulse carries the building through it and refills in the clean hours. Price belongs here, in the hour's plan.

When the hum dies, the Station carries the building. The same ear knows first. Two-way, as you said.

On 9 August 2019 the British grid changed key, B to A♯, and a million people lost power at that A♯; it replays here, transposed and labeled. The Station is simulated from Torus's published figures and labeled so.

Appendix

Methods and calibration

Crossings

Three bandpasses on the hum's harmonics, the loudest tracked. The period between rising zero crossings, interpolated, kept as a sample count plus a fraction so precision never decays. 100 ms and 1 s readings; the rate of fall over 300 ms.

Spectrum

A two-second Hann window, zero-padded through sonido-analysis's FFT, the peak interpolated with a parabola on log magnitude. THD from its ThdAnalyzer on the same two seconds. The cross-check.

Learned

A one-coefficient predictor, x[n] = a·x[n−1] − x[n−2], behind a four-pole low-pass, its coefficient learned by a gradient the compiler derives from the chain itself. Within 5 mHz of the stream's own zero crossings. The third witness.

Measured on a synthesized hum with noise at −30 dB: the one-second reading within 0.5 mHz, the spectral reading within 0.2 mHz, the rate of change under 0.023 Hz/s at rest, a trip at 0.20 Hz/s detected 250 ms after it starts. The interface's clock offset is read from the hum's long-run mean and printed on the page.
