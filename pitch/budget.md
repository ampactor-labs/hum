# The $10,000, line by line

Morgan Espitia · Mentl · Nucleus × Startup State open call, Hard Tech Week 2026

The plan: by day 30, the controller drives a real battery inverter on a bench. By day 60, with a site's written consent, it runs read-only beside a real battery for 14 days. It logs its decisions next to the site's own controls and sends no commands. By day 90, a pilot agreement that lets it send commands, and an SBIR proposal with Nucleus Grow.

Prices are from US retail listings and published cost guides found on 28 October 2026. A line marked "estimate" is my own figure, not a quote.
